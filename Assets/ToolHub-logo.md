# ToolHub logo

Selected concept: 01, red background and white chamfered T.
Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then converted to Windows ICO and Android PNG.

Final prompt: Reproduce the selected concept 01 red-square icon: a minimal white block capital T, broad straight horizontal rectangular head with only the upper-right corner diagonally chamfered, sturdy centered vertical rectangular stem, all remaining corners square. A uniform vermilion square background, clear margins, no text, gradients, shadows, texture, or other presentation-board elements.

`ToolHub.png` is the selected final artwork. `tools/build-icon.ps1` creates `ToolHub.ico` with 16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, 192 and 256 px frames, and the Android launcher PNG. The sidebar uses the same artwork as the desktop and tray icons.
